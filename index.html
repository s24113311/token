Concept #1

What is an LLM Token?

A Token is the atomic slice of text an AI processes. It is not strictly a whole word—it can be a subword fragment, whitespace, punctuation, or individual byte depending on vocabulary rules!

Concept #2

English vs Chinese Token Density

In English, 1 Token ≈ 3.5 to 4 characters (~0.75 words). In Chinese, 1 character often uses 1 to 2 tokens due to vocabulary frequency weight distribution in Byte-Pair Encoding!
